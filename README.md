## Executive Profile

I am a software developer based in Brasília, Brazil, focused on building complete digital products — from intuitive user interfaces to business logic, integrations, and data-driven experiences.

My work combines **TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node.js, and Python** to deliver responsive web platforms, analytical dashboards, automation workflows, and full-stack applications. I also work with **Java, relational databases, and TOTVS enterprise systems**.

> I turn complex requirements into clear, maintainable, and useful software.
